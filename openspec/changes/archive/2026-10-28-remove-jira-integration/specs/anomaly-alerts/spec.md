## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Integración de Webhooks de Jira / Slack
**Reason**: Third-party issue tracker integration (Jira Webhooks) is removed from the MVP scope and deferred to version 2 in accordance with pedagogical and project scoping guidelines focusing on the core FinOps happy path.
**Migration**: Users monitor budget burn and spending anomalies directly through the in-app Budget Warning Banners and the Notification Drawer without external ticketing dependencies.
